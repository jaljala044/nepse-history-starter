# Verified historical index bars

`backfill_ohlc.csv.gz` contains 28,230 NEPSE benchmark and sector index bars. Each Gregorian date appears in the reference trading calendar. Every added index/date key was absent across this archive's benchmark, sector, full OHLC and close-only files. Every accepted bar has positive ordered prices and exact matching non-flat OHLC across the two historical sources. Volume and turnover are absent from this supplement.

Sources were pinned to [SocrateAI f602a5b](https://github.com/socrateai-official/nepse-open-data/tree/f602a5b61e68582d28834d16f71151a4bae98d5b/ohlc_index), [YONEPSE 7b2bdea](https://github.com/Shubhamnpk/yonepse/tree/7b2bdea95f5c4aed987a772b8d1f87ddd097893f/data/indices/monthly), and [Nepse Decode e6e6fe7](https://github.com/srijankarki07/nepse-data/blob/e6e6fe77cd3378204c7938e66601cee2339a8b7a/data/sessions.json).
